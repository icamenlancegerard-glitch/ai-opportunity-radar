Freshness rule: evidence age is calculated from lastChecked. Fresh = 0–1 days, aging = 2–3 days, stale = 4+ days. A fresh source is not the same as a confirmed job. Recheck also inspects a bounded slice of source text for explicit availability evidence; it does not prove hiring, eligibility, compensation, or continued availability.
AI Opportunity Radar is an evidence-first prototype. Watch evidence you care about, inspect its recorded timeline, and use change alerts for follow-up. No fake certainty: freshness, reachability, and change detection help prioritize investigation; they do not claim hiring truth. Watchlist state is browser-local; alert subscriptions can be authenticated and server-backed in MVP 3.0.
